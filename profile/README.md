## How we work

| `01` Discover | `02` Design | `03` Build | `04` Run |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| We scope the real problem, not the feature list. Business goals first, architecture second. | System architecture and interface, designed together so the product holds up in production. | Tight iterations, working software every week. You see progress, not status reports. | We deploy on GCP, AWS or Vercel, then monitor and evolve what we ship. The product keeps working after the handshake. |

## What we believe

**Ship, then run.** A deliverable that nobody operates is a dead end. We deploy, then monitor and evolve what we build, so the product keeps working long after launch.

**Own the whole stack.** Backend, frontend, data, infrastructure. When one team owns everything between idea and production, nothing gets lost in the handoff.

**Speak your language.** Excel, SQL or strategy: we translate between business and engineering, so you always know what's being built and why.

**Simplicity wins.** The best system is the one your team actually uses. We cut manual work and complexity, not corners.

## DataSync

Our own ingestion platform, and the engine behind our clients' dashboards. Connectors for storefronts, marketplaces, 3PLs, ads, email and reviews land data in BigQuery on a schedule, and each client's syncs run in the client's own Google Cloud project, so the warehouse and the compute stay theirs.
